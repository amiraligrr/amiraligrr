## 🚀 Who am I?
Hey! I'm Amirali, a 15-year-old **builder, dreamer, and founder**. I believe the best way to predict the future is to build it yourself. Whether it's coding complex SaaS platforms, wiring up autonomous robots, or diving into cybersecurity, I’m always creating something new. 

I'm currently surviving the intense environment of SAMPAD (National Organization for Development of Exceptional Talents) at Allameh Helli High School. 

**Currently breaking limits:** I've already published **2 mobile apps** on Iranian stores (Myket & Bazaar) — and that number is growing fast. 

## 💻 What I'm Building

*   **🧠 LifeOS – Your Personal Operating System:** Why just live your life when you can optimize it? A comprehensive SaaS platform that tracks fitness, sleep, and study habits, powered by an AI-driven coach and gamified focus timers. *(Tech: Next.js, TypeScript, PHP, MySQL)*
*   **🏫 EduFlow & B2B Platforms:** Digitalizing the old-school way. A complete school management system (grades, homework, report cards) and a wholesale B2B platform connecting manufacturers directly to retailers.
*   **🌐 ZP-lang (Zereshk Polo Framework):** Because why code in English when you can code in Persian? A unique framework that lets you build programming languages using native keywords, seamlessly translating them to Python.
*   **🕹️ TapCoin:** A Persian "tap-to-earn" gaming ecosystem. Think dual-currency economies, passive income, and a full social network inside a mobile game. *(Tech: React Native, Node.js)*
*   **🏠 Argus & ASR (My Jarvis):** A local AI voice assistant (Python/Flask) that talks to my custom Arduino hardware layer to control my smart room, lights, and devices.
*   **🎮 MatchUp:** Real-time multiplayer gaming (cards, chess, billiards) with leaderboards and WebSocket chat. *(Tech: PHP, WebSockets)*
